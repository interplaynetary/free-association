# International Institutions

This directory contains research collaborations and partnerships with major international institutions and multilateral organizations.

## Structure

### UN System
- **UNDP**: UN Development Programme - sustainable development coordination
- **UNEP**: UN Environment Programme - environmental action and policy
- **UNFCCC**: Framework Convention on Climate Change - climate negotiations
- **UNESCO**: Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization - knowledge sharing
- **FAO**: Food and Agriculture Organization - food security
- **WHO**: World Health Organization - global health coordination
- **UNICEF**: Children's Fund - child welfare and development
- **IAEA**: International Atomic Energy Agency - nuclear cooperation

### Financial Institutions
- **World Bank**: Development financing and technical assistance
- **IMF**: International Monetary Fund - financial stability and cooperation
- **Regional Development Banks**: AfDB, ADB, IADB, EBRD - regional development

### Trade & Economic
- **WTO**: World Trade Organization - trade facilitation
- **OECD**: Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development

### Specialized Agencies
Other UN specialized agencies and international bodies
