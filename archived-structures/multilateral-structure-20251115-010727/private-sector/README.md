This directory contains research partnerships and collaborations with business entities across various sectors and scales.

### Startups & Innovation
Emerging technology companies, social ventures, and innovation labs

### Cooperatives

### Social Enterprises
Businesses with social or environmental missions

### Industry Associations
Sector-wide organizations and trade groups

### Financial Institutions
Banks, investment firms, and financial technology companies

### Technology Companies
Software, hardware, and platform companies

## Engagement Principles

### Transparency
Clear disclosure of business interests and funding sources

### Public Benefit
Alignment with public interest and social good

### Fair Competition
Support for competitive markets and innovation

### Worker Rights
Respect for labor standards and worker welfare

### Environmental Responsibility
Commitment to environmental sustainability

### Data Ethics
Responsible data governance and privacy protection

## Intellectual Property

### Open Source Preference
Encourage open-source contributions when possible

### Licensing Clarity
Clear licensing terms for all contributions

### Patent Pools
Exploration of patent pooling for public benefit technologies

## Contributing

Private sector entities should:

1. Declare relevant business interests
2. Separate commercial from research objectives
3. Support open standards and interoperability
4. Respect anti-trust and competition law
5. Engage constructively with other stakeholders
