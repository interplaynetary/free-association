## Research Standards

### Rigor
All research must meet high methodological standards and peer review

### Reproducibility
Studies should include sufficient detail for replication

### Open Access
Research should be openly accessible when possible

### Ethics
All research must follow established ethics protocols

### Collaboration
Multi-disciplinary and multi-stakeholder collaboration encouraged

## Publishing

### Preferred Licenses
- Creative Commons (CC BY or CC BY-SA)
- Open access for publications
- Open source for code and data

### Data Sharing
Research data should be shared following FAIR principles (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable)

### Pre-prints
Pre-print publication encouraged for rapid dissemination

## Contributing

Academic contributors should:

1. Follow established research ethics
2. Declare funding sources and conflicts of interest
3. Share datasets and methodologies
4. Engage with diverse stakeholder perspectives
5. Consider real-world implementation
