# Nations & National Governments

This directory contains country-specific research, policy implementations, and national coordination frameworks.

## Geographic Organization

### Regions
- **Africa**: 54 nations across diverse economic and ecological zones
- **Asia-Pacific**: Major economies and emerging markets
- **Europe**: EU members and regional partners
- **Latin America & Caribbean**: Regional integration efforts
- **Middle East**: Energy transition and diversification
- **North America**: Federal and provincial coordination

### Special Categories
- **Small Island Developing States (SIDS)**: Climate vulnerability and resilience
- **Landlocked Developing Countries (LLDCs)**: Trade and connectivity challenges
- **Least Developed Countries (LDCs)**: Development priorities and support

## Contributing

National government representatives should coordinate through official diplomatic channels. Researchers studying specific countries should:

1. Respect national sovereignty and sensitivities
2. Collaborate with local institutions when possible
3. Ensure data accuracy and cultural appropriateness
4. Follow established research ethics protocols

## Data Standards

Country data should follow ISO 3166 country codes and UN statistical standards.

## Privacy & Security

All nation-specific research must respect:
- National security considerations
- Privacy regulations (GDPR, etc.)
- Diplomatic protocols
- Cultural sensitivities
