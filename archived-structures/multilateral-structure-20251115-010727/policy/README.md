## Research Approach

### Evidence-Based
All policy recommendations grounded in empirical research

### Multi-Stakeholder
Consider perspectives from all affected parties

### Implementation-Focused
Address practical implementation challenges

### Context-Sensitive
Recognize diverse national contexts and capacities

### Rights-Based
Respect human rights and fundamental freedoms

## Contributing

Policy researchers should:

1. Maintain political neutrality
2. Declare funding sources and potential conflicts
3. Engage with diverse stakeholders
4. Consider implementation feasibility
5. Address equity and inclusion
6. Respect national sovereignty

## Policy Brief Format

Policy briefs should include:
- Executive summary
- Problem statement
- Policy options analysis
- Recommendations
- Implementation considerations
- Monitoring and evaluation framework
