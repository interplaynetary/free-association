# **Coalition Service Entity**

**Status:** Draft v0.1 (November 2025)  
**Part of:** Free Association Coalition Framework

---

## **Purpose**

A **Service Entity** provides Digital Public Goods (DPGs) and operational support to coalition members implementing voluntary coordination through Free Association principles.

| Role | Function |
|------|----------|
| **Coalition** | Network of member organizations experimenting with DPGs for voluntary coordination |
| **Service Entity** | Develops and maintains DPGs enabling automated cooperation and member sovereignty |

**Key insight:** Service Entity builds the rails; members run the trains.

The Service Entity enables the coalition's vision of **automation of cooperation** — where significant portions of capacity/resource allocation are automatically derived from network data, drastically reducing transaction costs and delays.

---

## **Services**

Service Entity develops DPGs supporting the four-part separation: **Publishing**, **Derivation**, **Recognition**, and **Allocation**.

### **Technical Infrastructure (DPG Development)**

Open-source implementations enabling:
- **Publishing**: Record format parsers, data validation, identity verification, subscription systems
- **Derivation**: Recognition engines, allocation algorithms, aggregation tools, network data processing
- **Recognition**: Mutual recognition calculation, organizational recognition derivation
- **Allocation**: Automated capacity distribution, resource matching, allocation transparency

### **Implementation Support**

Documentation, integration assistance, code libraries, testing environments, and developer resources enabling members to publish, derive, recognize, and allocate autonomously.

### **Coordination Platforms**

Communication channels, assembly logistics, knowledge management, and event coordination supporting inter-member collaboration while preserving sovereignty.

### **Research & Development**

Protocol refinement advancing automation of cooperation: network dynamics analysis, derivation algorithm improvements, pilot design support, impact assessment frameworks.

### **Capacity Building**

Member onboarding, training programs, peer learning facilitation, and mentorship helping organizations adopt voluntary coordination principles.

---

## **Governance**

### **Autonomy**

Service Entity governs itself: operational decisions, service delivery methods, technical choices, resource allocation, staffing, and organizational structure.

### **Accountability**

Service Entity commits to transparency, responsiveness to member feedback, adherence to coalition principles, equitable service to all members, and open-source core infrastructure.

### **Recognition-Based Accountability**

Members evaluate Service Entity performance through their recognition allocations. Underperformance leads to reduced recognition and resource allocation. Alternative service providers emerge naturally through member recognition.

| **Grants** | Foundation, government, or institutional funding for Digital Public Goods development |
| **Service Fees** | Free core DPGs; optional premium features, custom support, or training programs |
| **Hybrid** | Combination of grants, automated recognition-based allocation, service revenue, and volunteer contributions |

## **Principles**

Aligned with coalition implications of **Sovereignty and Interoperability** and **Automation of Cooperation**:

| Principle | Implementation |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Sovereignty** | Members retain full control over their data, recognitions, and priorities; options not mandates; enables self-hosting and member-run alternatives |
| **Interoperability** | All core DPGs use open standards, formats, and protocols; supports diverse technical implementations; forkable and community-contributable |
| **Automation** | Tools enable automated derivation and allocation from network data; reduces transaction costs and coordination overhead |
| **Transparency** | Public roadmaps, open financial reporting, documented decisions, accessible metrics; enables trust through visibility |
| **Accessibility** | Multi-language support, low-bandwidth tools, free basic tier; ensures broad participation regardless of resources |
| **Neutrality** | Equal service to all members; no preferential treatment; infrastructure focus enabling member choice |

## **Service Entity vs Members**

**Service Entity provides (DPG infrastructure):**
- Tools for publishing data (capacities, needs, recognitions, environmental data, etc.)
- Systems for deriving insights (recognition calculations, allocation algorithms, aggregations)
- Standards enabling interoperability and sovereignty
- Platforms facilitating coordination without requiring centralized control

**Members use infrastructure to:**
- Publish their own data and subscribe to others' data
- Derive allocations from network state
- Recognize each other's contributions
- Allocate resources through chosen protocols
- Implement pilots in their contexts
- Share knowledge and provide peer support

---

## **Multiple Service Entities**

Coalition architecture supports multiple Service Entities for resilience, innovation, specialization, and member sovereignty.

**Interoperability enables:**
- Service Entities recognize each other
- Members maintain awareness of available entities
- Shared DPG standards ensure compatibility across implementations
- Members allocate recognition across entities based on value received
- Competition drives continuous improvement

**Example specializations:** DPG technical infrastructure, capacity building, research, regional coordination, sector-specific support.

---

## **Relationship to Pilots**

**Service Entity does not:**
- Run pilot programs
- Make member allocation decisions
- Implement Free Association in specific contexts
- Deliver services to end beneficiaries

**Service Entity does:**
- Support members running pilots
- Document learnings and outcomes
- Provide implementation tools
- Facilitate knowledge sharing
- Help design pilot frameworks

Pilot ownership remains with members.

---

## **Evaluation**

### **Annual Process**

1. Service Entity publishes annual report
2. Members provide evaluations
3. Public discussion forum
4. Members adjust recognition

### **Continuous Feedback**

Member surveys, stakeholder meetings, real-time issue tracking, office hours, public roadmap with community input.

### **Performance Response**

Underperformance triggers member feedback, Entity improvement plan, monitoring period, and recognition adjustment. Members can reduce recognition, support alternatives, or withdraw recognition entirely.

| Category | Indicators |
|----------|-----------|
