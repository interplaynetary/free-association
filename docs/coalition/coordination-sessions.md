# **Informal Working Group Coordination Sessions**

**Status:** Draft v0.1 (November 2025)  
**COP30 Coordination:** November 10-21, 2025

## **Overview**

The Free Association Coalition is emerging through informal coordination sessions taking place across multiple venues during November 2025. These sessions are open to organizations interested in piloting Free Association as a coordination infrastructure.

## **Session Formats**

All sessions are designed for:

* **Exploration**: Understanding the Free Association framework
* **Experimentation**: Testing recognition and allocation concepts
* **Coordination**: Identifying potential coalition participants
* **Feedback**: Refining the participation framework

## **What to Expect**

### **First-Time Participants**

1. **Introduction** (30-45 min)
   * Overview of Free Association concepts
   * Recognition, derivation, allocation mechanics
   * Coalition participation framework

2. **Exploration** (45-60 min)
   * Interactive demonstration
   * Your organization's use case
   * Questions and discussion

3. **Next Steps** (15-30 min)
   * Coalition membership process
   * Technical implementation options
   * Follow-up coordination

### **Organizations Considering Membership**

1. **Deep Dive** (60-90 min)
   * Technical specifications
   * Secretariat governance structure
   * Decision-making protocols
   * Record format and registry

2. **Alignment Assessment** (30-45 min)
   * Your organization's needs vs. coalition capabilities
   * Interoperability considerations
   * Resource requirements

3. **Membership Process** (30 min)
   * Contact registration
   * Public key setup
   * Initial recognition declarations
   * First assembly participation

### **Working Group Sessions**

For organizations actively participating in framework development:

* **Framework Refinement**: Improving participation framework language
* **Protocol Design**: Developing decision-making protocols
* **Technical Specifications**: Record formats, derivation algorithms
* **Pilot Design**: Planning early implementation experiments
* **Secretariat Formation**: Coordinating founding member process

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

### **Do I need to commit to membership to attend?**

No. Sessions are open for exploration. Membership is a separate decision made after understanding the framework.

### **What if my organization can't attend any venue?**

Virtual sessions and asynchronous documentation allow full participation remotely.

### **Can I attend multiple sessions?**

Yes. Each venue has different focus areas and participant types. Attending multiple sessions provides broader perspective.

### **What's the cost to participate?**

Coordination sessions are free. Coalition membership has no fees—participation is based on mutual recognition and resource sharing, not financial contributions.

### **Will there be follow-up sessions?**

Yes. Post-COP30 coordination will continue through:
* Monthly virtual assemblies
* Regional coordination hubs
* Annual general assembly
* Working group sessions (as needed)

### **How do I prepare my organization for potential membership?**

1. Read the participation framework
2. Identify 3-5 organizations you recognize as aligned with your goals
3. Draft initial capacity and need statements
4. Generate PGP key pair for secure communication
5. Designate organizational representative for secretariat
