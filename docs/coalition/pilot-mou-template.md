## ABOUT THIS DECLARATION

This is **NOT** an agreement with a central coalition entity. The "Free Association Coalition" is an informal working group of organizations piloting coordination infrastructure under freedom of association principles.

**This Declaration establishes:**

- Participant's voluntary adoption of the Free Association Protocol for a defined pilot
- How Participant will engage with other coalition members
- Support arrangements available between coalition members on a voluntary basis
- Terms under which organizations coordinate with each other

**This Declaration does NOT:**

- Create a formal organization or legal entity called "the Coalition"
- Establish contractual relationships except as separately agreed between members
- Give any central body authority over Participant's decisions
- Require Participant to follow any specific allocation recommendations

## FREEDOM OF ASSOCIATION FRAMEWORK

### How Coalition Coordination Works

The Free Association Coalition operates as an informal working group under freedom of association principles:

**What "Coalition" Means:**
- An informal coordination space and community of practice
- A network of organizations voluntarily sharing infrastructure and knowledge
- A working group name, not a legal entity or contracting party
- Self-organizing coordination between independent organizations

**How Support Works:**
- Coalition members support each other voluntarily on a bilateral basis
- No central entity provides or guarantees services
- Organizations coordinate directly with each other as they see fit
- Specific support arrangements (hosting, development, etc.) are documented in separate bilateral agreements between specific organizations

**Why This Declaration:**
- Establishes Participant's intent to pilot the Protocol
- Describes how Participant will engage with coalition working group
- Documents pilot scope and evaluation approach
- Provides framework for internal approval and accountability
- Not a contract requiring counter-signature from a central authority

**Separate Bilateral Agreements:**

If Participant needs specific support services (infrastructure hosting, technical assistance, etc.), those arrangements should be documented in separate bilateral agreements with specific coalition member organizations that offer those services. This Declaration does not create those obligations.

## 3. PROTOCOL USAGE AND IMPLEMENTATION

### 3.1 How the Protocol Works

The Protocol generates **recommended allocation distributions** based on:

- Published priorities and capacities
- Mutual recognition patterns
- Partner-declared needs and capacities

**These recommendations are advisory only.** Participant reviews recommendations and decides whether to:

- Follow recommendations as presented
- Adjust recommendations based on constraints
- Override recommendations entirely
- Document rationale for decisions

### 3.2 Participant Responsibilities

Participant agrees to:

1. **Publish** (to defined network):
   - Organizational priorities and goals
   - Available capacities for pilot scope
   - Recognition allocations (distributed across partners)
   - Actual allocation decisions (for transparency)

2. **Review**:
   - Protocol-generated allocation recommendations
   - Mutual recognition calculations
   - Gap analyses and coordination opportunities

3. **Execute**:
   - Allocations through existing financial systems
   - Compliance with applicable procurement laws
   - Reporting through existing channels

4. **Adjust**:
   - Recognition patterns based on observed outcomes
   - Published priorities as circumstances change
   - Participation scope if needed

### 3.3 Voluntary Support Coordination Between Coalition Members

Coalition members may voluntarily support each other through:

1. **Technical Deployment** (self-organized):
   - Sharing open-source Protocol system
   - Infrastructure hosting (if members choose to offer)
   - Integration tools and documentation

2. **Peer Support** (voluntary):
   - Setup and configuration guidance
   - Partner onboarding assistance
   - Troubleshooting and technical knowledge sharing
   - Documentation and training materials

3. **Coordination** (member-facilitated):
   - Peer learning network participation
   - Coordination calls (optional, self-organized)
   - Best practice sharing across pilot cohort

4. **Infrastructure** (if applicable):
   - Hosting services (if member organizations offer)
   - System security and uptime (provider's responsibility)
   - Data privacy protections per applicable law
   - Protocol documentation updates (community-maintained)

**Freedom of Association Principle:** These support arrangements emerge from voluntary coordination between coalition members. No central entity coordinates or guarantees support. Organizations support each other as they see fit. Specific commitments may be documented in separate bilateral agreements.

## 9. COALITION AS INFORMAL WORKING GROUP

### 9.1 What "Coalition" Means

The "Free Association Coalition" is an **informal coordination space** for organizations piloting the Protocol under freedom of association principles. It is:

- A working group name, not a legal entity
- A community of practice, not a formal organization
- A peer network, not a hierarchical structure
- Self-organizing, not centrally governed

The Coalition **is NOT:**

- A legal entity or formal organization with bylaws
- A contracting party or signatory to agreements
- A procurement vehicle or funding mechanism
- A decision-making body with authority over members
- Subject to membership fees or financial obligations
- An entity that can own property, hold accounts, or employ staff

### 9.2 How Coalition Members Coordinate

Organizations participating in the coalition working group may:

- Share experiences and best practices
- Coordinate technical development (voluntary)
- Propose protocol improvements (community-driven)
- Facilitate peer learning networks
- Collaborate on documentation and research
- Support each other under freedom of association
- Organize coordination sessions and calls

**All coordination is voluntary and self-organized.** No central entity coordinates activities or makes decisions for members.

### 9.3 Participation Rights and Non-Obligations

As a coalition member (working group participant), [ORGANIZATION]:

**May:**
- Participate in discussions and coordination calls
- Propose protocol improvements and contribute code
- Access peer learning and technical resources
- Co-author documentation and research
- Facilitate support for other members
- Use "coalition member" descriptor publicly

**Does NOT Have To:**
- Vote on any positions or decisions
- Make public statements of support
- Contribute financially or provide resources
- Implement recommendations from other members
- Align with other participants' priorities
- Attend meetings or participate actively
- Support the Protocol beyond pilot period

### 9.4 Freedom of Association Principle

Coalition coordination operates under freedom of association:

- Organizations join and leave voluntarily
- Participation level is self-determined
- Support arrangements are bilateral (not centrally managed)
- No hierarchy or central authority
- Consensus-seeking, not majority rule
- Dissent and experimentation are welcomed
- Fork-friendly: Organizations may create alternative versions
